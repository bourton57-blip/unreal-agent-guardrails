# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being precise and honest about what works and what does not.
- Criticising code and decisions, never people.
- Accepting correction gracefully, and correcting your own mistakes in public.
- Being mindful that this project serves developers with widely varying levels
  of Unreal Engine experience.

Behaviour that is unacceptable:

- Harassment or intimidation of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Insults, or sexualised language or attention.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and whenever someone represents the project
in public.

## Reporting

Do not contact maintainers through personal channels, and do not publish
allegations publicly before they have been reviewed.

- **For a security vulnerability:** use the private channel described in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting).
- **For any other concern, including a conduct report:** open a regular issue
  on this repository. A conduct report may be marked as confidential in its
  title so that discussion can move to a private channel.

No e-mail address is published in this repository, by design.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported
through the channels above. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be
respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and
will take appropriate fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they
deem inappropriate. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other
maintainers.
